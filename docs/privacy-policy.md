# PrisM Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 September 2026

PrisM — Işık Atölyesi is designed as an offline puzzle game.

## Data collection
The current release does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data. It contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, social login, cloud account system or in-app purchase SDK.

## Local data
Game progress and preferences are stored locally on the device. This includes completed level identifiers, the last played level, audio/haptic preferences and visual-quality preference. This information is not transmitted by the game.

## Permissions and network use
The current release does not require location, contacts, camera, microphone or account access. Core gameplay does not require an internet connection.

Google Play itself and the operating system may process platform-level information under their own terms when the app is downloaded, installed, updated or reviewed. That processing is outside the PrisM game code.

## Children
The current store positioning is for a general 13+ puzzle audience and is not specifically directed to children under 13. If the target audience is changed, the applicable Google Play Families requirements and this policy must be reviewed before release.

## Changes
If analytics, crash reporting, advertisements, online services, cloud save, account systems or purchases are added later, this policy and the Google Play Data Safety declaration must be updated before that version is released.
